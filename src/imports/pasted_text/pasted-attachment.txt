Create a premium, modern, professional web-based business management application for:

LEGENDARY ONE TECHNOLOGIES

Tagline:
Where Innovation Meets Excellence

The application is an internal CRM + ERP + Project Management + Employee Management + Billing system for an IT and digital services company.

The company handles around 30–40 clients and provides services including:

• Website Development
• Software Development
• Mobile App Development
• Graphic / Poster Design
• Video Editing
• Digital Marketing
• Organic Marketing
• Branding
• Maintenance and other IT services

The software should allow the owner to manage the entire company from one place.

==================================================
DESIGN STYLE
==================================================

Create a polished 2026 SaaS-style interface.

The UI should feel premium, professional, simple and production-ready.

Visual direction:

• Clean light theme
• White cards
• Very light grey background
• Deep blue / indigo primary colour
• Cyan / teal secondary accent
• Green for success
• Amber for warning
• Red only for overdue/errors
• Dark charcoal text
• Modern professional typography
• Soft shadows
• 12–16px rounded corners
• Clean icons
• Spacious layouts
• Strong hierarchy
• Subtle animations and interactions

Use inspiration from modern SaaS tools such as:

Stripe
Linear
HubSpot
ClickUp
Notion
Zoho

Do not directly copy them.

Avoid:

• Clutter
• Excessive gradients
• Too many colours
• Cartoon styling
• Huge unnecessary cards
• Old-fashioned admin-dashboard appearance

Use reusable Figma components and Auto Layout throughout.

==================================================
IMPORTANT UX RULE
==================================================

Do not place every available metric on every screen.

Prioritize information.

Each page should have:

• One clear page title
• 3–5 important summary metrics maximum
• Primary action button
• Search / filtering when required
• Main table, cards or workspace
• Secondary information below

Use progressive disclosure:

Simple overview first.

Detailed information should appear inside tabs, drawers, modals or detail screens.

FINAL PRODUCT VISION
==================================================

This should feel like a custom business operating system for Legendary One Technologies.

The owner should be able to immediately understand:

How many clients are active?

What projects are currently running?

What work has been delivered?

How much should clients pay?

How much money has been received?

What payments are pending?

What invoices are overdue?

What is monthly revenue?

What are company expenses?

What is current profit?

Which employee worked on each project?

How many hours did they work?

Who is present or absent?

How much salary has been paid?

What quotations, invoices and documents belong to each client?

What requires attention today?

Create a highly polished, realistic and production-ready SaaS interface.

Use:

₹ Indian Rupee

DD/MM/YYYY date format

Indian GST terminology

Realistic Indian business data

The product should feel powerful but very easy to understand.

Do not make individual UI elements unnecessarily large.

Keep cards, icons, tables, buttons, inputs and typography at balanced professional sizes.

Aim for medium-density information presentation suitable for regular office use on a laptop or desktop.
